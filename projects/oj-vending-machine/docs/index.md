## Why it's hard

Automatic juice machines exist, but they are hard to run well. The juicer itself is the easy part. The hard parts are what surround it:

| Challenge | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Waste handling | Peel is roughly half the fruit by weight and spoils within a day |
| Food-contact hygiene | Blades, chute and nozzle need cleaning that actually works |
| Siting | Power, refrigeration and a drain all in one place |
| Input cost | Several oranges per cup, with seasonal price swings |

## Design focus

The engineering that sets this machine apart is the **self-sanitizing dispense path** and a **waste system** that stretches the time between service visits, not the juicing mechanism.

## Safety

The design follows **IEC 60335**, the international safety standard for household and similar electrical appliances: protection against electric shock, moving parts, heating and abnormal operation.
