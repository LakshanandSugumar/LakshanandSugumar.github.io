# Sensor & HMI Subsystem — Power Budget

**Lakshanand Sugumar — Team 302**

---

## Overview

This page presents the complete power budget for the Sensor & HMI subsystem of the R6 Recon Amphibot. The analysis verifies that all active components on the 3.3 V rail are accounted for, total peak current is calculated under worst-case conditions, a safety margin is applied, and the selected regulator can safely supply the required load.

The subsystem operates from a single regulated **3.3 V rail** generated by the **AP63203WU-7 synchronous buck regulator** (3 A rated). In the final V1 system, 3.3 V is supplied to this board via the upstream ribbon cable connector from the ESP32 board's onboard regulator. The AP63203WU-7 on this board provides local regulation when the board is powered independently during development and testing.
